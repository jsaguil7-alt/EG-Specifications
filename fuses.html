The 1992-1995 Honda CIvic fuse system protects the car's wiring and controls everything about the car. Depending on your trim model, you might only have the underhood and underdash fuse box. If your car is equipped with ABS, you will have an additional fuse box located under the hood near the passenger side fender and airbox that controls all your ABS components. Please try to use the correct fuse and try not to put a bigger amp fuse if you are having electrical issues. This can create a fire hazard. We are here to keep these cars going not destroy them.
