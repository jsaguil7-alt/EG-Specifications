Torque Specifications

This page provides torque specifications for the 1992–1995 Honda Civic. Front passenger and front driver use the same specifications, Rear driver and rear passenger use the same specifications. Please use a torque wrench when tightening bolts. Safety is key to maintaining these cars. If you don't know how to use a torque wrench please look up videos online or use your torque wrench instructions.

Front Suspension

Component Torque Specification
Upper control arm mounting nuts 47 lb-ft
Upper ball joint castle nut 29–35 lb-ft
Lower ball joint castle nut 36–43 lb-ft
Front lower control arm bushing bolt 47 lb-ft
Front compliance bushing bracket bolts 60 lb-ft
Damper fork bolt 47 lb-ft
Damper pinch bolt 32 lb-ft
Upper shock/damper mounting nuts 22–28 lb-ft
Tie-rod end nut 32–33 lb-ft
Front axle spindle nut 134 lb-ft
Front wheel lug nuts 80 lb-ft

Rear Suspension

Component Torque Specification
Rear compensator arm bolt 47 lb-ft
Rear lower arm bolt 40 lb-ft
Rear lower control arm bolts 40–47 lb-ft
Lower shock absorber bolt 40 lb-ft
Upper shock absorber mounting nuts 28 lb-ft
Radius arm to frame bushing bolt 64 lb-ft
Radius arm to hub carrier, inner bolt 40 lb-ft
Radius arm to hub carrier, outer bolt 74 lb-ft
Rear trailing arm to frame 47 lb-ft
Rear upper arm 29 lb-ft
Rear stabilizer bar bolts 16 lb-ft
Rear spindle nut 134 lb-ft
Rear wheel lug nuts 80 lb-ft

Flywheel bolts (manual transmission) 87 lb-ft
Intake manifold nuts 16 lb-ft
Valve cover nuts 7 lb-ft
Oil pan bolts 17 lb-ft
Engine oil drain plug 33 lb-ft

Rocker arm assembly bolts (8 mm) 16 lb-ft
