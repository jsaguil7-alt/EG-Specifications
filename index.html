WEBSITE DESCRIPTION

EG CIVIC SPECIFICATIONS is a one stop website with everything you would need to keep this generation of golden era Hondas going. It includes information about factory torque specifications, fuse box diagrams, ECU pinouts, check-engine light codes, fluid capacities and body configurations, with much more information to come. It provides useful information for owners, enthusiasts, and DIY mechanics looking to maintain, repair, and better understand their vehicles.

If you have any questions or feedback feel free to contact us.
