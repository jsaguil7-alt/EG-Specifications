Check Engine Light Codes

Procedure:

Locate the 2-pin service check connector located near the passenger footwell, underneath the dash near the ECU.
Bridge the two pins using a jumper. You can use a piece of wire or for an easier application, use a paper clip. Make sure both sides of the pin connect to create a solid connection between them.
Now look at your gauge cluster and look for the flashing check engine light symbol.
Every long flash is an increment of 10, and every fast flash is an increment of 1.
Example: 2 long flashes followed by 2 quick flashes indicates code 22, which is VTEC oil pressure switch.
Codes are meant to get you in the right direction. It does not necessarily mean the sensor is bad, further diagnosis is needed.

  • 0 ECM - Electronic Control Module
  • 1 O2A - Oxygen sensor #1
  • 2 O2B - Oxygen sensor #2
  • 3 MAP - Manifold absolute pressure sensor
  • 4 CKP - Crank position sensor
  • 5 MAP - Manifold absolute pressure sensor
  • 6 ECT - Engine coolant temperature sensor
  • 7 TPS - Throttle position sensor
  • 8 TDC - Top dead center sensor
  • 9 CYP - Cylinder sensor
  • 10 IAT - Intake air temperature sensor
  • 12 EGR - Exhaust gas recirculation lift sensor
  • 13 BARO - Atmospheric pressure sensor
  • 14 IAC (EACV) - Idle air control valve
  • 15 Ignition output signal
  • 16 Fuel injectors
  • 17 VSS - Speed sensor
  • 19 Automatic transmission lockup control solenoid valve
  • 20 Electrical load detector
  • 21 VTEC solenoid valve
  • 22 VTEC oil pressure switch
  • 23 Knock sensor
  • 30 Automatic transmission A signal
  • 31 Automatic transmission B signal
  • 36 Traction control (JDM)
  • 41 Primary oxygen sensor heater
  • 43 Fuel supply system
  • 45 Fuel system too rich or lean
  • 48 LAF - Lean air fuel sensor
  • 54 CKF - Crank fluctuation sensor
  • 58 TDC sensor #2
  • 61 Primary oxygen sensor
  • 63 Secondary oxygen sensor
  • 65 Secondary oxygen sensor heater
  • 71 Misfire cylinder 1
  • 72 Misfire cylinder 2
  • 73 Misfire cylinder 3
  • 74 Misfire cylinder 4
